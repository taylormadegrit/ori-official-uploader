Privacy Policy — Ori Official Uploader

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What the app accesses

The app requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It uses this permission solely to upload videos that the channel owner has chosen to publish to their own YouTube channel.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read, collect, or store personal information, messages, comments, or analytics.
  • It does not change channel settings or delete content.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

Data storage

The only thing stored is the Google sign-in token needed to upload, kept privately on the operator's own computer. It is never shared.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
